# Pixel Medieval Market Props (16x16)
62 transparent 16x16 pixel-art props: market stalls, containers, food and goods, trade items, street furniture.
- PNG/16x16 (native) and PNG/64x64_x4 (nearest-neighbour), grouped by category
- spritesheet.png (+ x4) and spritesheet.json (x, y, w, h per frame)
Engines: Unity, Godot, GameMaker - use Point (no filter), no compression.
